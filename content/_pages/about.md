title: About
---
Hannah is currently the Well-Being Communities Advisor at the Community Sector Council of Newfoundland and Labrador. 

Her writing has appeared in the *Hamilton Review of Books* and *Cyclista Zine*, among other publications. In 2024, she won the Short Works Prize for Poetry from *Hamilton Arts & Letters Magazine* and the Hamilton Public Library.

Her research has been published in the *Journal of Law and Social Policy*, the *Journal of Technology and Human Services*, and the *Journal of International Women’s Studies*. 

Hannah holds an M.A. in Gender Studies and Feminist Research from McMaster University and a B.A. in Sociology and Gender and Women’s Studies from the University of King’s College/Dalhousie University.
